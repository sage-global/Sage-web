## A Milestone in Practical RF Engineering Education

The valedictory ceremony of the SAGE–RVCE–Keysight Hands-on Workshop marked a triumph for hands-on engineering education in India. 39 participants successfully simulated, fabricated, and tested GaAs FET RF amplifiers for 5G communication bands.

<Quote
  content="Watching students test their own fabricated circuits using Keysight network analyzers and seeing real-world S-parameters match theoretical models is the pinnacle of engineering learning."
  author="Dr. K. D. Nayak"
  cite="Former Director General, DRDO & SAGE Executive Board"
/>

The program demonstrated the tremendous impact of collaborative efforts uniting academic institutions, global software and instrumentation leaders, and seasoned scientific mentors.
