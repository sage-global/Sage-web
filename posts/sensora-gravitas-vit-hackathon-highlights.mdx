## 36 Hours of Non-Stop Innovation at VIT Vellore

Under the flagship annual tech festival graVITas '25 at Vellore Institute of Technology, SENSORA brought together over 200 engineering enthusiasts for an intensive 36-hour hackathon and technology symposium.

Supported by title sponsor SAGE and ecosystem partner StartupTN, student teams developed working hardware prototypes across biomedical instrumentation, smart sensor meshes, and wireless telemetry systems.
