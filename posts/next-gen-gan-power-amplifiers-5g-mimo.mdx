## Revolutionizing High-Power 5G RF Transmission

Gallium Nitride on Silicon Carbide (GaN-on-SiC) has emerged as the premier semiconductor technology for base-station power amplifiers. Offering superior breakdown voltages, saturated electron velocity, and thermal conductivity compared to traditional Silicon LDMOS, GaN enables high power density in compact Massive MIMO arrays.

### Key Focus Areas:

1. **Doherty PA Configurations:** Optimizing back-off efficiency for signals with high Peak-to-Average Power Ratios (PAPR).
2. **Digital Pre-Distortion (DPD):** Linearizing non-linear amplifier responses to comply with stringent EVM and ACLR standards.
3. **Advanced Packaging:** Minimizing parasitic inductances and enhancing heat dissipation under continuous high-power operation.
