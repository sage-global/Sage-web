## Convening Global Minds in Wireless Engineering

The First SAGE International Symposium on Advances in Wireless Technologies brought together leading researchers and industry veterans to discuss 5G/6G communication systems, energy harvesting, and semiconductor architectures at Prestige Falcon Tower in Bengaluru.

Keynote sessions covered high-efficiency supply modulated amplifiers, RF energy harvesting methodologies at IIT Kanpur, and state-of-the-art SoC design principles.
