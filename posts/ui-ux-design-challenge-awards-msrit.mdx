## Celebrating Creative Interfaces in Engineering Software

The Awards Ceremony of the SAGE UI/UX Design Challenge, held at Ramaiah Institute of Technology in Bengaluru in collaboration with IEEE Bangalore Section, brought together students, designers, and industry mentors.

Finalists presented interactive prototypes and intuitive user dashboards tailored for complex microwave measurement and simulation workflows, demonstrating how design thinking enhances technical software usability.
