## A Visionary Initiative for Engineering Excellence

Shastry Associates Global Enterprises (SAGE) was formally launched at a grand ceremony in Bengaluru, graced by eminent dignitaries including Mr. A. S. Kiran Kumar (Former Secretary, Department of Space) and Mr. Sanjay Nayak (Co-Founder & Former CEO, Tejas Networks).

Founded to foster high-level mentorship, practical engineering workshops, and global academic-industrial partnerships, SAGE aims to catalyze India's evolution into a premier technology product nation.
