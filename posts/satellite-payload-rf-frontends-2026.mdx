## Transforming Space-Ground Connectivity

Low Earth Orbit (LEO) mega-constellations are transforming global internet distribution, disaster communication, and earth observation. Designing space-qualified RF hardware demands stringent size, weight, power, and cost (SWaP-C) optimization.

### Key Innovations:

1. **Beamforming ICs (BFICs):** Silicon-based multi-channel beamformers reducing payload mass.
2. **Radiation-Hardened RFICs:** Ensuring operational reliability over multi-year mission lifetimes.
3. **Direct RF Sampling ADCs/DACs:** Simplifying receiver architectures by digitizing at microwave frequencies.
