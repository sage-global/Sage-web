## Where Microwave Engineering Meets Quantum Physics

Superconducting qubits operate at millikelvin temperatures and rely on microwave pulses in the 4 GHz to 8 GHz range for state initialization, manipulation, and measurement. Precision microwave engineering is essential for scaling quantum computer architectures.

### Critical Engineering Elements:

- **Cryogenic High-Electron-Mobility Transistor (HEMT) LNAs:** Achieving noise temperatures approaching the quantum limit.
- **Parametric Amplification:** Josephson Parametric Amplifiers (JPAs) for single-shot readout fidelity.
- **Crosstalk Suppression:** Micro-machined shields and superconducting transmission lines to prevent qubit decoherence.
