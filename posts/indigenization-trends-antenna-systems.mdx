## Building Self-Reliance in High-Performance Antenna Systems

Achieving self-reliance in high-reliability airborne and naval antenna systems requires seamless coordination between electromagnetics simulation, specialized substrate manufacturing, and rigorous environmental stress screening.

Industry and defense researchers from CSIR-NAL and SAGE shared vital insights into radome compensation, conformal phased array packaging, and EMI/EMC compliance testing.
