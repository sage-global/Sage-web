## Advancing Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems in Microwave Engineering

The two-day National Symposium on RF MEMS jointly organized by SAGE and BMS Institute of Technology in Bengaluru focused on cutting-edge design, micromachining, and packaging of RF switches and phase shifters for satellite communications and radar systems.

Participants had the exclusive opportunity to visit the SITAR DRDO cleanroom facilities, gaining first-hand insight into semiconductor fabrication protocols and MEMS reliability screening.
