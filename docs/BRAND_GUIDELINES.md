# SAGE — Brand Guidelines & Content Master Document

> **Organization:** Shastry Associates Global Enterprises, LLC (SAGE)  
> **Brand Standard:** 2026 Master Specification  
> **Domain:** Applied Electromagnetics, RF, Microwave & Wireless Systems Engineering

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 Mission & Vision

* **Mission:** *To disseminate knowledge and information in the area of applied electromagnetics and wireless systems.*
* **Vision:** *To be the leader in disseminating knowledge and information in radio frequency wireless systems engineering and technologies globally.*
* **Promise:** Practical, mathematically rigorous engineering knowledge delivered by veteran industry leaders and academic fellows.
* **Positioning:** SAGE is not a generic e-learning portal. It is a specialist engineering institute and advisory network bridging graduate theory with the industrial lab bench.

### 1.2 Brand Voice & Personality

| Trait | Definition | Execution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Precise** | Grounded in mathematical rigor and real lab measurements. | No marketing fluff. Every claim supported by engineering experience. |
| **Accessible** | Complex electromagnetic concepts made intuitive. | Clean design guidelines, link budget cheat-sheets, and step-by-step derivations. |
| **Professional** | Clean, structured, high-authority presentation. | Tailored for serious engineers, graduate researchers, and R&D managers. |
| **Global** | Collaborative network spanning multiple continents. | Associates across India, United States, South Korea, and Europe. |
| **Practical** | Theory seamlessly applied to physical hardware. | Circuit schematics, PCB layout tips, and measurement techniques. |

## 3. Brand Assets & Imagery Guidelines

* **Logo**: The SAGE orange hexagon emblem (`public/Shastryhexagon(Orange).png`) paired with the clean white/ink typography wordmark.
* **Photography Direction**:
  * Real engineering labs, spectrum analyzers, vector network analyzers, oscilloscopes, and circuit boards.
  * Professional, high-resolution portrait headshots of faculty and corporate fellows.
  * Antennas, mmWave satellite infrastructure, and telecom towers for wireless communications content.
* **No Generic Stock**: Avoid artificial "handshake stock" or overly staged office photography. Focus on authentic bench testing and engineering collaboration.
